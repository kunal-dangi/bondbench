# Code of conduct

bondbench follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, critique ideas
rather than people, and keep discussion on topic. Harassment, personal attacks
and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any
other project space.

To report a problem privately, message the maintainer on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rohan-jindal1999/). Reports are
handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments, close
threads or block accounts that break these rules.
